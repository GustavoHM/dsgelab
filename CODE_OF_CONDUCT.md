# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other space the project runs.

In short: be respectful and constructive, criticise work rather than people, and assume good
faith. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at gustavo@gakusai.net. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer decides on the response, following the enforcement guidelines of
the Contributor Covenant, from a private warning to a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
